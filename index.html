Features

Everything you glance at while driving, in one place

Big, calm and easy to hit on a car touch screen. Dark and light themes, a moving cockpit background and glass panels that pick up the accent colour.

Full screen while driving

From version 3.0 the car keeps the full-screen layout while you drive, so the big map, the clock and the music stay on the whole screen for the entire trip instead of shrinking when you set off.

Clock and weather

A big light clock, the date, live weather with the day's high and low, and your next three calendar events. A small gauge shows battery, Wi-Fi and mobile signal, and glows green while the phone charges. Weather comes from Google or free Open-Meteo and follows your location if you choose no city. Tap the clock for 12 or 24 hours, the weather to refresh it.

Music

Shows what is playing in any music app: cover art, title, progress you can drag, previous, play and next, and a like button when the app supports it. The buttons are frosted glass, and behind them a twisting ribbon of lines in the two main colours of the album cover moves with the music. The accent colour can follow the cover too.

Live map

Google's map with 3D buildings and live traffic follows your car, in a dark night style after sunset. In Russia and nearby you can choose Yandex's map instead. It zooms out as you speed up, tilts when you drive, and you can pinch, drag, turn it with two fingers and snap back to your position. Expand it to fill the whole screen. The night style can be set to Auto (by daylight), Theme, On or Off.

Navigation

Search a place (results appear while you type), pick Home or Work, or choose a spot on the map. See the route options with live traffic times, add up to four stops and choose an alternative right on the map: the route you pick is the one you drive. While you drive, the map is Google's own navigation screen: Google's blue arrow and route line, its turn banner with the small "Then" banner below it, and its arrival card, each set in frosted glass. Leave the route and a new one is found at once. Live traffic colours stay on the route, the buttons fade away until you touch the map, and after a few drives the way you usually take is offered first as "Your usual route" (learned only on your phone). With the Yandex map you get the route and its travel time, but no turn-by-turn, because Yandex's free licence does not include it.

Weather you can see

A sky in the clock card that fades softly at the glass edge, rain that runs down the glass in drops, snow that piles up on the cards and storms with lightning. The weather never changes your theme or the map, and you can try any condition with Weather animation override. Switch it off in the settings if you prefer a plain look.

Nearby EV chargers

Tap the bolt on the map and green charger pins appear around you, with the nearest one chosen at once. Step through the others with the bar at the bottom, see the drive time and connector types, and tap Go. While you are driving, the bolt adds the charger as a stop before your destination, and cancelling brings the old route back.

App dock

Your favourite apps one tap away, opened on the car screen, including work profile and dual apps. Tap the all-apps button to see every app and search them. Press and hold the dock to edit it: the icons wobble, drag to reorder, tap the badge to remove, and add more with the plus button.

Floating return button

When you open another app on the car screen, a small glass button stays on top. Tap it to come straight back to the cockpit. Drag it anywhere you like.

Liquid glass design

Frosted panels with a lit top edge, a soft shadow below and an accent glow along the rim, also around the live map. Choose a background, an accent colour or your own photo, in a dark or a light theme that follows the phone or the sun and fades smoothly when it switches.

Fits every screen

One layout for the phone and two for the car: a compact landscape and a roomy full screen. Sizes are scaled so buttons and text look the same on each.

Your language

English, Khmer, Russian and Polish, including dates, units, the map and the turn-by-turn directions. More can be added.

Phone notifications

A frosted glass card shows your phone's notifications on the car screen. Swipe it up or tap outside to dismiss it, or turn it off in the settings.

Guided setup

The first start walks you through your language, your look (theme, accent colour and animations), the permissions and your map service, with a clear screen about Google's costs and the key you need. Have a backup? Restore it during the setup and everything is back at once. You can run the setup again from the settings whenever you like.

Private by design

No account, no advertising, no analytics. Your keys and settings stay on your phone, Google's costs need your consent first, and the privacy page lists everything that is sent and to whom.

Report and request

Something wrong, or an idea? Send a report or a feature request from the settings in a few taps, with screenshots and the log. You can follow it on GitHub afterwards.

Screens

Three layouts, picked for you

The app looks at the window it is shown in and arranges the cards to fit. Nothing scrolls; everything stays within reach.

Download and install

Up and running in a few minutes

Xiaomi Cockpit is not on an app store, so you install the APK file yourself. It takes about five minutes the first time.

Before you install: what is required

Xiaomi Cockpit is made for the Xiaomi phone-to-car connection. To use it in the car you need:

  • A supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region (China-region system software).
  • Xiaomi interconnectivity that connects to your car, with the same Xiaomi account on the phone and in the car.

Without interconnectivity the app can only be opened on your phone. It will not show on the car screen.

What you need

  • A supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region, with HyperOS and Android 12 or newer.
  • A Xiaomi car, and Xiaomi interconnectivity that connects the phone to the car, with the same Xiaomi account on both.
  • Baidu Maps and Mi Dashboard must not be installed on the phone (see the FAQ).

Optional: a Google key

The live map works right away. For routes, destination search, traffic times, turn-by-turn guidance, chargers and Google's weather you add your own Google API key once. Weather works without it too, with any map service. The setup guide and the user guide show every click.

How to get a key

Prefer Yandex maps? Add a free Yandex MapKit key instead: how to get a Yandex key.

  1. Download the APK on your phone

    Tap the download button above in your phone's browser. If your browser warns that this type of file can be harmful, choose to keep it. It is an app file that does not come from a store.

  2. Install it

    Open the downloaded file. Android asks you to allow installing apps from this source: allow it, then tap Install. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway. Installing a newer version over an old one keeps your settings, and the app can fetch and install updates itself (Settings > Updates). Coming from version 2? Version 3.0 installs as a new app, so back up your settings first (see the FAQ).

  3. Open the app and choose your language

    The first start is a guided setup: your language, your look, the permissions, then your map service (Google, Yandex or None) with a short screen about Google's costs and your key. If you saved a backup before, the setup offers to restore it right after the permissions and then ends there. You can change all of it later in the settings.

  4. Allow what the app needs

    On the setup screen tap each row and allow it:

    • Display over other apps: for the floating return button.
    • Music and navigation access: so the app can show what is playing.
    • Notifications, Location (speed and map) and Calendar (next events).

    If a switch is greyed out, open App info, tap the three-dot menu and choose Unlock permissions, then try again. On HyperOS also turn on Autostart, set Battery saver to No restrictions, and allow Display pop-up windows and Chain start.

  5. Connect to the car

    Connect your phone to the car with Xiaomi interconnectivity (required for the car screen) and open Xiaomi Cockpit from the app list on the car screen, or pin it to the car's home screen. The dashboard fits the window it is shown in.

  6. Set your city and your places

    Open the settings (swipe down on the dashboard and tap the gear). Choose your city for the weather (or leave it empty and the weather follows your location) and set Home and Work. Add and arrange your dock apps on the dashboard itself by pressing and holding the dock. The user guide explains every setting.

Questions

Good to know

Does it work without Xiaomi interconnectivity?

Only on the phone. Without interconnectivity the app can be opened on your phone, but it cannot be shown on the car screen. To use it in the car you need a supported Xiaomi phone in the Chinese region that can connect to the car with Xiaomi interconnectivity.

Is it free? Is there an account or advertising?

It is free, community built, and has no account and no advertising.

Why does the app use the package name com.baidu.BaiduMap?

The car only lists apps it knows, and it only lets Baidu Maps keep the full screen while the car is moving. So from version 3.0 Xiaomi Cockpit uses the Baidu Maps package name, and the dashboard stays full screen on the move. The side effect is that it cannot be on the same phone as the real Baidu Maps or Mi Dashboard (which uses the same name): if one of them is installed, Android refuses the install with a "conflicting signatures" message. Uninstall them first. Versions before 3.0 used com.baidu.searchbox.

I have version 2. How do I move to 3.0?

Version 3.0 has a new package name, so it installs as a new app and does not update the old one (the old app's update check says the update is for a different app). Move once: (1) in the old app open Settings > Backup > Back up settings, choose With my API keys and save the file; (2) uninstall the old Xiaomi Cockpit; (3) uninstall Baidu Maps and Mi Dashboard if they are on the phone; (4) install the new APK; (5) in the setup, on Restore a backup?, tap Choose backup file and pick the file. If your Google key is restricted to Android apps, add the package name com.baidu.BaiduMap to it in Google Cloud. See the guide.

Do I need a Google API key?

Not to see the map. The live Google map is built in. The key is needed for routes, search and traffic times (so Home, Work and search work), for turn-by-turn guidance, for the nearby chargers' details and for Google's weather. Without it weather comes from Open-Meteo and chargers from OpenStreetMap. Google gives a free monthly allowance and charges your own account above it only after you accept that in the app; the key stays on your phone. See the guide.

Can I use Yandex maps instead of Google?

Yes. In Settings > Service > API service choose Yandex and add your own free Yandex MapKit key. You get Yandex's live map with traffic, place search, nearby chargers and a route preview with Yandex's travel time. There is no turn-by-turn guidance, because Yandex's free licence does not include it. See the guide.

Play Store wants to "update" the app to Baidu. What should I do?

Install the newest Xiaomi Cockpit. Because the app uses a Baidu package name, Google Play may offer the real Baidu app as an update. Xiaomi Cockpit has a much higher internal version number, so the prompt goes away. Do not accept that "update".

How do I move my settings to a new phone or a new install?

On the old phone open Settings > Backup > Back up settings and save the file, for example in Google Drive. On the new install the setup asks Restore a backup? after the permissions: tap Choose backup file and pick it. Your settings, dock, Home and Work, recent places, background photo and learned routes come back; Android asks for the permissions again. See the guide.

The floating button does not bring the cockpit back

Tap it once and wait about two seconds. Make sure Display over other apps is allowed and Autostart is on for the app. More tips are in the troubleshooting section.

Something does not work. How do I report it?

Open Settings > Support > Report a problem in the app, describe the problem and add screenshots. It opens a public report you can follow, and the screenshots and full log stay private. See Support.

Can I ask for a new feature?

Yes. Open Settings > Support > Request a feature in the app and describe your idea. It opens a public request you can follow.

Is it made by Xiaomi?

No. It is an independent community project and is not affiliated with Xiaomi, Baidu or Google. Names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Support

Found a problem? Report it from inside the app

No account and no typing of logs by hand: describe what went wrong, add screenshots and the app sends everything the developer needs. You can follow your report on GitHub afterwards.

How to report

  • Swipe down on the dashboard and tap the gear to open the settings.
  • Under Support, tap Report a problem.
  • Describe what happened, add screenshots and keep Include the app log on.
  • Check the preview and tap Send. You get a report number and a link to follow it.

What is shared

  • Public: your description, the app and phone details and the last lines of the log.
  • Private: the screenshots and the full log. Only the developer can open them.
  • Removed first: your Google key, Home and Work addresses, positions and e-mail addresses.
  • You see the public text before sending, and nothing leaves the phone until you tap Send.
